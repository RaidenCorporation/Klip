For those out there feeling bored, stressed, or just something on your mind irritating you? Thats when Klip comes in—a fun, cool antistress game.

# 🖥️ Platform Availability
Klip is only available as an APK for Android.

# 📩 How To Download
1. Look for the latest APK from the latest release on the Releases tab.
2. Scroll down on the latest release to see “Assets”.
3. In the Assets section, look for app-release.apk and click it.
4. It will install.
5. Go to your regular Files app.
6. Look for app-release.apk.
7. Once you found the APK file, press the 3 dots ⋮ or hold the file and press "Install".
8. It will be installed to your device. Enjoy!
